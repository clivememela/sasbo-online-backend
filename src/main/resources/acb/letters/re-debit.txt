## What happens next

Under the debit order rules, we may present this debit order again next month, on {{debitDayPhrase}}, together with
that month's subscription.

When your debit day falls on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, your debit order goes off on the next working day.
At the end of the month it moves back instead, to the last working day of the month, so it never goes into the next
month.

## What you need to do

- Please make sure there are enough funds in your account for both debits.
- If your banking details have changed, please send us your new details.

Your bank may charge a fee for an unpaid debit order; SASBO is not responsible for it.

Under SASBO's constitution, a membership ends when the subscription has not been paid for three months. If that
happens, we will write to let you know.
