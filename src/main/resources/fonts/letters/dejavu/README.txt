DejaVu Sans -- the letters' fallback, glyph by glyph
================================================

WHY
A member's name can hold a letter the letters' typeface does not have (TeX Gyre Adventor has no ḓ, the Tshivenda
d with a dot below). Such a letter is printed from DejaVu Sans, one glyph at a time, rather than as '?' (register
A68). A character neither font has refuses the letter, naming its code point and nothing of the member.
